Fashion Styles & Inspiration

Bohemian Boho Fashion

Exploring the Bohemian Style

Bohemian style is inspired by free-spirited fashion, earthy colors, flowy dresses, crochet textures, and natural accessories.

Preppy Classic Fashion

The Classic Preppy Look

Preppy style features neat and classic clothing, including collared shirts, cardigans, pleated skirts, blazers, and loafers.

Chic Minimalist Fashion

How to Achieve a Chic Style

Chic style focuses on elegant and coordinated outfits, neutral colors, tailored blazers, trousers, and minimal accessories.

Texas Style Western Fashion

Discovering Texas Western Fashion

Texas style is inspired by Western fashion, featuring denim jeans, cowboy boots, cowboy hats, plaid shirts, and statement belts.

About Fashion Notes

Fashion Notes is a fashion inspiration blog dedicated to helping you discover and express your personal style. Whether you're interested in bohemian elegance, classic preppy aesthetics, minimalist chic, or western fashion, we have something for everyone.

Our mission is to provide stylish outfit ideas, fashion tips, and styling advice to help you build a wardrobe that reflects your unique personality and lifestyle.

This blog is created as part of an academic project for "Achieving Operational Excellence: Creating a Simple Blog" course, showcasing modern web design and development practices.
